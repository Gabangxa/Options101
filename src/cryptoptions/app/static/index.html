What this means

Waiting costs a buyer money: every day the option loses some value even if the price stands still. This is called time decay, or theta. Being right too late is the most common way option buyers lose.

What this means

How much movement the market expects. When it rises, every option you hold is worth more, and when it falls they are worth less, even with no price change. It often drops sharply after a big event, which is called an IV crush. This lever is held at zero unless you move it.
